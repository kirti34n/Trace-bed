# Support

No supported service, support channel, response-time objective, or named support owner is
published for this repository. Do not infer support from source availability, issue templates, or
an automated check.

For local exploration, read [README.md](README.md), [docs/CAPABILITIES.md](docs/CAPABILITIES.md),
and [docs/OPERATIONS.md](docs/OPERATIONS.md). For deployment-specific questions, use the channel
provided by the organization operating that deployment.

Before offering public support, a human organization must publish the supported versions, contact
route, data-handling expectations, escalation path, and service boundaries it can actually honor.

Candidate build artifacts and their release evidence are not supported releases.
They remain review material until an accountable release authority publishes a
supported-version and support policy.
