# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participate respectfully and professionally. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate
disclosure of sensitive information, and disruptive conduct are not acceptable in project spaces.
Assume good intent where reasonable, focus criticism on ideas and changes, and respect boundaries
around personal and operational data.

## Scope

This applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and other project spaces operated
by an eventual accountable organization.

## Reporting and enforcement limitation

This repository does not currently publish a conduct contact or identify an enforcement team. Do
not invent one. Before operating a public community, a human organization must publish a private
reporting path, response expectations, enforcement authority, and an appeal process.

Until then, use the private escalation process of the organization operating the relevant space.
Do not place sensitive reports in public issues. This document cannot promise an investigation or
a response without named, accountable human maintainers.
